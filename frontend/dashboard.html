  • Child is lethargic, unusually sleepy, unconscious, or unresponsive

17 • Important Limitations & Clinical Safety Notice
Scope and Technical Limitations:
  • Screening result is not a definitive diagnosis: Anthropometric indicators flag potential risk, but qualified clinical evaluation is required.
  • Measurement accuracy is essential: Accurate measurement of age, length/height, and weight is required for valid Z-scores.
  • Decision-support only: ChildNutri AI does not prescribe medicines, antibiotics, or treatments.
Clinical Safety Notice: ChildNutri AI provides screening and clinical decision-support information. It does not provide a medical diagnosis and does not replace assessment by a qualified healthcare professional. Clinical management should follow current applicable clinical guidelines and local health authority protocols.
