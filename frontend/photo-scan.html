📋 Best Photo Guidelines for Highest Accuracy

  • ✓ Clear Torso & Chest: Ensure the upper chest and abdominal wall are visible (minimal thick clothing).
  • ✓ Frontal Posture: Face and upper limbs facing the camera directly without steep angles.
  • ✓ Even Ambient Lighting: Bright room or daylight. Avoid deep harsh backlighting or heavy flash shadows.
  • ✓ Target Population: Infants & toddlers 0–59 months. School-aged children cannot be evaluated with under-5 algorithms.
