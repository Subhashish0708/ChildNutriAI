Capabilities

Engineered for Pediatric Decision Support

Combining deep learning computer vision, WHO growth tables, and explainability to empower frontline health workers.

Multimodal AI Screening
Fuses convolutional visual embeddings from clinical photographs with precise physical measurements for deep phenotype screening.
WHO Growth Standards
Computes age- and sex-specific LMS Z-scores for Height-for-Age (HAZ), Weight-for-Age (WAZ), and Weight-for-Height (WHZ).
Explainable AI (XAI)
SHAP feature contribution bars explain tabular decisions; Grad-CAM visual heatmaps identify anatomical areas of AI attention.
Longitudinal Tracking
Continuous growth velocity monitoring across repeat visits with proactive early warning alerts for weight faltering.
Future Risk Forecasting
Predictive 30-, 60-, and 90-day trajectory modeling allowing frontline health officers to intervene before acute deterioration.
Automated Health Dossiers
One-click generation of official clinical PDF reports with complete diagnostic traces, meal guidance, and referral notes.
Protocol

The 5-Step Pediatric Assessment Protocol

A streamlined clinical workflow engineered for field health workers, Anganwadis, and pediatric clinics.

01
Patient Identification
Select infant from cohort registry or record demographic profile and birth history.
02
Physical Anthropometrics
Record verified weight, recumbent length, and MUAC arm circumference measurements.
03
Clinical Photo Ingestion
Upload patient image for EfficientNet-B0 visual tensor feature extraction.
04
Live WHO Calculations
Instant mathematical benchmark against WHO international reference curves.
05
Synthesis & Dossier
Multimodal risk score, SHAP attributions, Grad-CAM attention map, and PDF export.
