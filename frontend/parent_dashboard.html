Understanding Your Child's Growth

Weight-for-Age (Weight)

Indicates overall body mass relative to children of the same age and sex. Helps detect if an infant is underweight.

Height / Length-for-Age (Height)

Reflects linear growth over time. Consistently low height relative to age indicates stunting, which is linked to chronic nutritional deficiency.

MUAC (Mid-Upper Arm Circumference)

Measures muscle mass and subcutaneous fat in the upper arm. It is the primary field indicator for detecting acute malnutrition quickly and accurately.

WHZ (Weight-for-Height)

Compares current weight directly with current height. Helps identify wasting (sharp weight loss) independent of age.
